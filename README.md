## Fuerza

**A workout log and AI coach, live on the App Store.**

Spend the workout lifting, not managing the workout. You walk in with the plan ready and log a set in one tap. The coach sets the next weight and reps from your history, and it always shows why.

[fuerza.spencerstrei.com](https://fuerza.spencerstrei.com/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fuerza-workout-log-coach/id6778350211)

SwiftUI · on-device Apple Foundation Models · English and Spanish · free, no account, no data collected
